# Retention Payback

Winning a customer costs money. Each month they stay, they bring some profit back. Enter the cost to win one customer, the profit you make from them in a month, and the share still buying next month. The page shows how many months it takes to earn back the cost, and roughly what a customer is worth over their life.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies, no network calls.

- **Verdict:** how many months it takes to pay back the cost, or whether it never does.
- **Shows:** months to pay back, the lifetime profit per customer, and a table for the first two years.
- **Arithmetic:** the page explains it in plain words, and the formulas are in the How it works section.
- **Share:** a paper-ground PNG card, and the URL hash carries your numbers.

Retention is held steady, which is the simplest case. Example figures are made up.
